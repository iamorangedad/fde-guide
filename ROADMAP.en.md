## Electives: Sections 3.3–3.9 of Chapter 3 (they do not replace the 12 weeks above)

Sections 3.1–3.2 are already scheduled in W5–W7. The sections below live in the [02-ai-llm](02-ai-llm/README.en.md) directory. They are electives within Chapter 3, not a separate set of chapter numbers, and they do not replace the hands-on deliverables of Chapters 1–4. Schedule them only when job descriptions mention training, fine-tuning, CUDA, or inference serving. As before, part-time learners can double each item's duration. Verify the CUDA material yourself once you have a GPU; this repository was not verified on a GPU when it was written.
