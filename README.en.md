# Becoming an FDE (Forward Deployed Engineer): A Systematic Course

> 🌐 [中文](README.md)

This repository is a systematic, textbook-style course rather than a single article. Each chapter contains: learning objectives, core concepts and definitions, principles, examples, diagrams (Mermaid), case studies, exercises (with reference answers or scoring rubrics), a summary, key terms, and further reading (verified sources only).

## Who It Is For
- Software engineers with 1+ years of programming experience who want to move into a hybrid "engineering + customer" role
- Solutions engineers (SEs), technical consultants, and data analysts who want to round out their engineering skills
- Upper-year students in computer science and related majors

## How to Use It
1. Start with `00-overview` to build an accurate understanding of the role;
2. Then read the [Preliminary Chapter: Linux Fundamentals](01-foundations/00-linux-basics.en.md) (it takes no chapter number), and move on to Chapters 1 and 2;
3. Follow the 12-week or 24-week plan in `ROADMAP.md`;
4. Do every chapter's exercises hands-on; the work in `04-projects` is the core evidence for your job search;
5. Concentrate on `05-interview` in the two weeks before interviews;
6. Chapter 3 lives in the directory `02-ai-llm` (directory numbers are not chapter numbers). On the 12-week roadmap, read 3.1–3.2. Read 3.3–3.9 only if the role involves model training or inference performance; those sections are electives, not required for every FDE.

## Study Plan Overview
- **12 weeks (full-time, intensive)**: weeks 1–2 overview, Linux fundamentals, and integration; weeks 3–4 deployment and security; weeks 5–7 LLM systems; week 8 customer skills; weeks 9–11 projects; week 12 interviews.
- **24 weeks (while employed)**: double the length of each phase, and practice customer skills in parallel during the project phase.
See [ROADMAP.en.md](ROADMAP.en.md) for details. Read Sections 3.1–3.2 in weeks 5–7; 3.3–3.9 are electives within the same chapter and do not replace the 12 weeks above.
