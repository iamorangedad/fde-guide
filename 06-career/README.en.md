## 7.2 Resume
Write each experience as "customer/business problem → what you built → measurable result". Highlight: cross-system integration, shipping to production, working directly with customers, LLM projects and their evaluation.

## 7.3 Portfolio
GitHub repositories + demo videos + one-page case studies (problem, solution, results, retrospective).

## 7.4 Negotiation
Negotiate only after you have a written offer; understand the components of the total package (cash, equity, travel allowances); base your case on multiple offers and market data, and stay professional.

## 7.5 Growth
First 90 days: learn the platform, shadow senior FDEs, independently deliver a small milestone, and write your first product feedback. Long term: choose a main direction among technical depth, domain knowledge (finance/healthcare/government), and team leadership.
